## Why this project exists

Thailand has large amounts of public economic data spread across agencies, release schedules, formats, revisions, and analytical traditions. Human teams have limited time and attention, while economic conditions change continuously.

Thailand Economic OS exists to build a continuously operating, evidence-grounded system that can ingest public data, maintain historical vintages, measure the current economy, test competing explanations, forecast future conditions, simulate policy scenarios, challenge its own conclusions, and learn from prediction errors.

The goal is **not** to create a chatbot that sounds like an economist.

The goal is to create an auditable economic intelligence infrastructure whose measurable analytical capabilities can improve over time.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Every important conclusion must trace back to evidence.**
2. **Every forecast must be recorded before the actual outcome is known.**
3. **UNKNOWN ≠ PASS.**
4. **LLMs do not create numerical truth.**
5. **Historical vintages must be preserved.**
6. **Models compete against real out-of-sample evidence.**
7. **Uncertainty must be visible, not hidden.**
8. **Critical conclusions require challenge / Red Team review.**
9. **The system must learn from prediction error.**
10. **Autonomy must never exceed auditability.**
11. **Human beings retain authority over normative social/policy values.**
12. **One active version at a time (WIP = 1).**

## Anti-drift question

Before starting any substantial task, ask:

> “If this task succeeds, which capability in the chain above becomes more trustworthy or possible?”

If the answer is unclear, the task belongs in the Parking Lot until its strategic role is defined.

## North Star success condition

The project succeeds when the system is measurably better at:
- sensing the current state of the Thai economy;
- producing calibrated forecasts;
- detecting turning points and risks;
- explaining competing causal hypotheses;
- simulating policy trade-offs;
- preserving full evidence/audit trails;
- improving through prediction-error feedback;

while remaining reproducible, transparent, and subject to human policy authority.
