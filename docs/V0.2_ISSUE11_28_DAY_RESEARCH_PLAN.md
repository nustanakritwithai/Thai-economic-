- **DAY**
- **SOURCE**
- **QUESTION** — what exactly are we trying to answer?

- **EVIDENCE** — official docs/page/request/file used.
- **FINDING** — what was learned?
- **CONFIDENCE** — HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW.
- **UNKNOWN** — what remains unresolved?
- **IMPACT** — effect on connector/data-contract design.
- **NEXT QUESTION** — one question for the next research step.
